Overview

Panori Hotel presents a compelling investment opportunity in one of Tanzania’s fastest-growing coastal regions. Established in 1990, this well-known, family-operated hotel is located in the sought-after Raskazone area of Tanga, just 3 kilometres from the city centre and moments from the Indian Ocean.

Set on a generous, low-density plot, the property offers a rare balance of proven operational performance and future redevelopment potential. With a long-standing reputation, loyal guest base, and consistent trading history, Panori delivers both immediate income and clear scope for value growth.
